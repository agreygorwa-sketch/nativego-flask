Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

NativeGo ("we", "us") connects tourists with verified local providers across Kenya. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have. If you have questions, contact us at hello@nativego.co.ke.

1. What we collect

  • Account data: your name, email address and a securely hashed password when you sign up.
  • Provider listings: business name, category, location, description, daily rate and phone number you choose to publish.
  • Bookings: trip dates, group size and notes you share when requesting a booking.
  • Reviews & favourites: ratings, comments and saved providers.
  • Technical data: login timestamps and basic security logs (e.g. failed login attempts) used to protect accounts.

2. Why we collect it

  • To create and secure your account.
  • To display provider listings to travellers and process booking requests.
  • To verify provider identities and keep the marketplace trustworthy.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse (e.g. login rate-limiting).

We do not collect payment details — this demo version processes no real payments.

3. What we never do

We never sell your personal data. We do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. Provider contact details are shown only to facilitate bookings you initiate.

4. Cookies & sessions

We use a single essential session cookie to keep you logged in (HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax, expiring after 2 hours of inactivity). We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

5. Data retention

We keep your account data for as long as your account is active. You may ask us to delete your account and personal data at any time by emailing hello@nativego.co.ke; we will remove it within 30 days, keeping only records we are legally required to retain.

6. Your rights

You may request a copy of the data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Contact hello@nativego.co.ke and we will respond within 30 days.

7. Security

Passwords are stored as one-way hashes (never plaintext). All forms are protected against cross-site request forgery, and logins are rate-limited. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the platform evolves. Material changes will be announced on the site, and the "last updated" date above will change.
