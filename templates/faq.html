Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about booking — and earning — with NativeGo.

🧳 For tourists

How does booking work?

Browse verified providers, open a profile, pick your date and group size, and send a booking request. The provider confirms (usually within a day), and you sort the finer details directly with them. Your request starts as pending and moves to accepted once confirmed.

Do I pay through NativeGo?

Not yet — NativeGo is currently a demo, so no real payments are processed on the platform. You arrange payment directly with your provider (M-Pesa, cash or bank transfer, as you agree). When we launch in-app payments, we'll announce it here first.

What is the cancellation policy?

You can cancel any pending request yourself from My bookings at no cost. Once a provider has accepted, please contact them directly — we recommend giving at least 48 hours' notice so they can rebook the slot.

Are providers really verified?

Yes. Every provider goes through our verification: ID check, phone confirmation and a manual review by our admin team before their listing goes live. Look for the ✓ Verified badge on every listing.

What if my provider cancels?

It happens rarely, but if it does, contact us and we'll help you find an alternative provider for your dates. Providers who cancel repeatedly lose their verified badge.

Is it safe to meet a provider?

We verify every provider, but stay street-smart: meet in a public place first, share your itinerary with someone you trust, and keep communication on the platform until you meet. Read our Trust & Safety page for the full guide.

Can I leave a review?

Absolutely — reviews are the heart of NativeGo. After your experience, open the provider's profile and leave a 1–5 star rating with a comment. Honest reviews help other travellers and reward great providers.

Can I book for a group?

Yes. Enter your group size (up to 50) when sending a request, and mention any special needs in the notes — providers regularly handle school groups, corporate teams and wedding parties.

🧭 For providers

How do I join NativeGo?

Sign up as a provider and tell us about your business — what you offer, where, and your daily rate. Your application goes into our verification queue.

What does verification involve, and how long does it take?

We check your ID, confirm your phone number and manually review your listing for quality. Most applications are reviewed within 2–3 business days. You'll be notified as soon as you're approved and live.

What are the fees?

Listing is free — no upfront costs, no subscriptions. NativeGo takes a 10% commission only on completed bookings. If you don't earn, we don't earn.

How do payouts work?

NativeGo is currently a demo with no in-app payments, so you collect payment directly from your clients for now (M-Pesa works great). When platform payments launch, payouts will go straight to your M-Pesa or bank account.

Can I edit my listing?

Anytime. From your Dashboard you can update your description, price, phone number and location. Changes go live immediately.

What if I need to cancel a booking?

Life happens — decline or message the tourist as early as possible from your dashboard. Repeated last-minute cancellations can cost you your verified badge, so keep your calendar honest.
