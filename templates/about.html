How it works

Two sides, one simple flow.

🧳 For travellers

  1. Discover — search by destination, category or keyword. Every provider is verified before listing.
  2. Book — send a request with your date and group size. The provider confirms, usually within a day.
  3. Experience — meet your expert, enjoy Kenya like a local, then leave a review to help the next traveller.

🧭 For providers

  1. Sign up free — create an account and describe your services, location and daily rate.
  2. Get verified — our team reviews every application. Verified listings go live for travellers to book.
  3. Earn directly — accept requests from your dashboard, deliver great experiences, build your rating.
