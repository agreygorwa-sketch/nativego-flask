Trust & Safety

How we keep NativeGo safe for travellers and providers alike.

How verification works

No one goes live without passing all three checks.

1

ID check

Every provider submits a valid national ID or passport, which our team reviews manually.

2

Phone confirmation

We confirm the provider's phone number so travellers can always reach a real person.

3

Admin review

Our team reviews the listing itself — services, pricing, photos — before awarding the ✓ Verified badge.

Safety tips for tourists

  • Meet in public first. Start at a hotel lobby, park gate or other public spot — not a private address.
  • Share your itinerary. Tell a friend or your hotel where you're going, with whom, and when you expect to be back.
  • Check the badge. Only book providers with the ✓ Verified badge, and read their reviews first.
  • Keep chat on NativeGo until you've met in person — it keeps a record if anything goes wrong.
  • Trust your gut. If something feels off, cancel and tell us. We'd rather rebook you than risk it.
  • Agree the price upfront. Confirm the total cost and what's included before the experience starts.

Provider code of conduct

Verified providers agree to:

  • Honour confirmed bookings, or cancel with as much notice as possible.
  • Be honest about what's included — no surprise fees on the day.
  • Treat every traveller with respect, regardless of background.
  • Keep travellers' personal details private — never share them.
  • Put safety first: proper equipment, sensible routes, no unnecessary risks.

Something wrong?

Report a safety concern and our team will prioritise it — we respond within 24 hours.
